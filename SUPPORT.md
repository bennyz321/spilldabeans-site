Need help with Spill Da Beans, or want to report a problem? Email us at [support@spilldabeans.com](mailto:support@spilldabeans.com).

To help us sort it out quickly, tell us:

- the phone number or handle on your account
- what you were trying to do, and what happened instead
- your iPhone model and iOS version

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in the app: **Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account**. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address above and we'll help.
